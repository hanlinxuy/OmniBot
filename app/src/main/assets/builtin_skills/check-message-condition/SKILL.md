# Check a message condition

Identify the condition that controls the requested action. Search results, titles and inbox previews locate candidate messages; they do not establish the condition. Open relevant messages and read their full bodies, including qualifications, pending steps and requirements. Acknowledging receipt of a request does not establish its approval or completion.

When delegating reading to a GUI tool, ask it to open the candidate message and report the relevant body with its qualifying language, without sending anything. A report that a matching title is visible is incomplete evidence. If a separate confirmation could exist, inspect the relevant search results or requested mailbox scope before concluding that it is absent. Read an attachment when the condition depends on its contents, rather than inferring from its name.

Distinguish established true, established false and unknown. Perform only the follow-up authorized by the established condition. If the needed content cannot be read, report that limitation; do not treat unread content as absence or fabricate a confirmation. If no follow-up is required, do not send an unsolicited status message.

After an authorized send or reply, inspect its result before reporting completion. Do not repeat an uncertain send. Describe the state actually established: a request forwarded for review is not an approval, and a tool's successful inspection is not proof of the underlying business condition. This workflow does not authorize access to unrelated messages or applications.
